Frequently asked questions

Does the app need an internet connection?
No. The whole guide is inside the app. Only the official source links open web pages, in Safari.

Where is my progress stored? Can I move it to a new phone?
Only on your device. It is included in your normal iPhone or iPad backup and restore.

How do I start over?
Open Settings (gear icon on the Guide tab) and tap Reset progress.

How do I change the language?
Settings → Language: System, English or Türkçe. Technical terms and code stay in English in both.

A source link opens a search instead of a page.
That source could not be verified when the guide was written, so the app searches developer.apple.com for its exact title rather than guessing a URL.
