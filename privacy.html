Privacy Policy

Field Guide · Effective 16 November 2026

Field Guide is published by Cavid Abbasaliyev ("we", "us").

The short version

The app collects no data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

What stays on your device

The app remembers which sections you marked as read, your checklist answers, the plan weeks you finished, the last chapter you opened and your language setting. This is stored only on your device, in the app's own storage. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app, or tapping Settings → Reset progress, removes it.

Network

The whole guide is included in the app and works offline. The app itself makes no network requests. When you tap an official source link, it opens in Safari; Apple's and other websites' own privacy policies apply there.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy: see the support page.
